Who We Are

Aqua Dive Egypt is a professional diving center dedicated to creating unforgettable underwater experiences in the Red Sea. We welcome beginners, certified divers, and adventure seekers from around the world.

Our experienced instructors provide internationally recognized diving courses, guided dives, and personalized training while maintaining the highest standards of safety and professionalism.

Whether you're taking your first breath underwater or continuing your diving journey, we're here to help you discover the beauty of Egypt's marine life with confidence and excitement.
